பூதக்கண் ணாடியிற் புகுந்திலன் போதுளன் வேதக்கண் ணாடியில் வேறே வெளிப்படு நீதிக்கண் ணாடி நினைவார் மனத்துளன் கீதக்கண் ணாடியிற் கேட்டுநின் றேனே.
He is Seen in the Mind-Mirror
TIRUMANTIRAM
2986
He appears not in the glasses of the fleshy eye; He is in the Lotus of the Heart;
He appears in the glass of Vedic Jnana;
He is in the mind-glass of the righteous that think of Him,
Him I saw in the glass of songs
Listening to that Divine Music,
I enraptured stand.
நாமமோ ராயிரம் ஓதுமின் நாதனை
ஏமமோ ராயிரத் துள்ளே இசைவீர்கள் ஓமமோ ராயிரம் ஒதவல் லாரவர் காமமோ ராயிரங் கண்டொழிந் தாரே.
Chant "Aum" Incessantly
2987
Chant, the Lord's name, a thousand times, A thousand blessings shall yours be;
They who chant the lovely "Aum" a thousand times,
Are rid of a thousand thousand passions, away.

Ajapa
Jnana that is our Refuge;

Constant in my thought;

Hearken you!
I chant the One Letter (Aum), Dear to our Lord.

He came and stood before His devotees, He the Rare Truth;
Even if the Celestials and their King Indra ask What will they get, but the music of lovely damsels?
But that which devotees get is beyond all their heaven.

Still Your Thoughts and Be Purified Like muddied water these men's minds are, Unclear in vision,
They see not and say, 'This is Lord;" From the heart's lake within,
Draw a pitcherful,
And keep it in stillness apart; When you thus purify it,
You shall become Siva.
மெய்த்தவத் தானை விரும்பும் ஒருவர்க்குக் கைத்தலஞ் சேர்தரு நெல்லிக் கனியொக்குஞ் சுத்தனைத் தூய்நெறி யாய்நின்ற தேவர்கள் அத்தனை நாடி அமைந்தொழிந் தேனே. Yearn For Him
He of the Penance Pure;
Transparent as amla fruit
On the palm of those who yearn for Him; He the Pure One,
Whom Celestials seek in ways righteous;
Him, my Lord, I sought;
And thus ever remained.
